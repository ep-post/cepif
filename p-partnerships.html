Focus Areas

What we do in this pillar

CSR Engagement

Helping corporate CSR teams design and fund programs that genuinely address migrant worker welfare — with strong monitoring, reporting, and impact visibility.

NGO Strengthening

Building the institutional capacity of frontline organisations — through training, mentoring, MIS systems, documentation support, and access to funding networks.

Government Collaboration

Engaging state labour departments, BOCW boards, migration management units, and welfare boards to strengthen policy implementation and inter-state coordination.

Partnership Models

How we work with partners

CSR Implementation Partner
We design and implement CSR programs for companies wanting to address migrant worker welfare in their supply chains or operating regions — with full compliance reporting.
Capacity Building Provider
We deliver training, workshops, and mentoring for NGO staff on program design, M&E, documentation, and fundraising — individually or in cohorts.
Government Technical Advisor
We provide research, analysis, and implementation support to government bodies working on migration governance, scheme portability, and labour welfare.
Network Convener
We convene multi-stakeholder platforms that bring together government, industry, civil society, and community voices around shared problems — creating the conditions for systemic change.
