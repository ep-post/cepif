CEPI Foundation generates primary research on migration, labour markets, and social exclusion — designed to inform policy, programs, and advocacy, not just academic archives.

Research Themes

What we study

🗺️

Internal Migration in India

Primary research on migration patterns, corridors, push and pull factors, and the lived experiences of workers — generating data that is currently absent from public policy discourse.

⚖️

Labour Market Analysis

Analysis of informal labour markets, wage structures, working conditions, and employer practices — providing evidence for policy reform and industry engagement.

🧒

Children & Education

Research on education outcomes for children in migrant households — documenting the specific barriers to continuity and the conditions under which children thrive despite migration.

📱

Technology & Digital Inclusion

Testing and evaluating digital tools for worker registration, service delivery, and scheme access in low-connectivity, low-literacy field contexts.

🏛️

Policy & Governance

Analysis of existing migration governance frameworks, scheme portability, inter-state coordination mechanisms, and gaps in implementation — to support evidence-based advocacy with state and central government.

Methodology

How we conduct research

Participatory Research
We prioritise methods that centre the voice of migrant workers — FGDs, in-depth interviews, and community-based data collection — rather than purely extractive approaches.
Mixed Methods
We combine quantitative surveys with qualitative fieldwork to produce findings that are both statistically grounded and contextually rich.
Action Research
We embed research within our program work — treating implementation as a learning process and documenting what we discover along the way.
Open Access
We publish findings openly wherever possible to contribute to the shared knowledge base on internal migration in India.
