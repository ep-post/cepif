Our Areas of Work

Where we focus.

01 Safe Migration & Worker Wellbeing Supporting migrant workers through access to information, services, and opportunities before, during, and after migration.
02 Children of Migrant Workers Promoting education continuity, learning support, and child wellbeing for children in migrant households.
03 Skills & Employability Creating pathways to decent work through skilling, industry partnerships, and career development.
04 Research & Innovation Generating evidence and testing scalable solutions for migration governance and labour market gaps.
05 Partnerships & Capacity Mobilising governments, employers, CSR partners, and civil society for systemic change.
