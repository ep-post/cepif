Our Story

Why CEPI was formed

CEPI Foundation — Catalysing Empower People Initiatives Foundation — was established as a Section 8 company in 2024 to provide a dedicated institutional home for innovation, research, and systems-level work in the field of migrant labour welfare.

It operates in close collaboration with Empower People, the anti-trafficking and social empowerment NGO with roots going back to 1999 and formally constituted in 2006 — bringing deep community trust, field presence across Delhi, Haryana, Bihar, and Assam, and a proven track record of survivor-centred work.

Where Empower People focuses on direct service delivery and anti-trafficking work, CEPI is designed to catalyse: to incubate new approaches, generate evidence, engage CSR and institutional funders, and build the capacity of others working in adjacent fields.

Together, the two organisations form an ecosystem — one rooted in community, one oriented toward systems change.

What We Stand For

Our values

⚖️

Equity

We believe that economic migration should not mean exclusion from rights, services, or dignity. Equity is the lens through which all our work is designed.

🔬

Evidence

Our programs are grounded in research. We generate evidence, question assumptions, and commit to learning from what doesn't work as much as what does.

🔗

Collaboration

No single organisation can address the scale of migration-related exclusion alone. We are deliberate ecosystem builders — connecting government, CSR, civil society, and communities.

💡

Innovation

We test new approaches, use technology thoughtfully, and look for solutions that can be adopted and scaled by others — not just within our own programs.

🌱

Dignity

Every program we design starts from the premise that the people we serve are rights-holders, not beneficiaries. Dignity is not a program output — it is a precondition.

📈

Scale

We are oriented toward solutions that can reach beyond our own footprint — through policy influence, replication, and strengthening others working in this space.

Vision

A society where migrant workers and their families have equitable access to opportunity, education, dignity, and social inclusion.

Mission

To catalyse innovative solutions that improve economic mobility, educational outcomes, and wellbeing for migrant workers and their families.
